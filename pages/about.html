Why Frontier?

Based in Mooresville, North Carolina and founded in 2023, Frontier Motorsports exists to make grassroots racing more accessible, more competitive, and more connected. We believe a race team should be more than a logo on a kart—it should be a place where drivers develop, families belong, and partners see real value.
